## 1. 01 · The problem — slide 2

**Canvas** 1248 × 1512 · **File** `img/art-01-problem.png`

**Prompt**

```
NO background, fully transparent canvas, no paper - flat cut-paper illustration, riso-print logic. The artwork is the subjects only, cut out cleanly, floating on a fully transparent canvas - there is no paper, no backdrop, no rectangle, no frame, no border, no vignette, no ground plane and no horizon; every pixel that is not part of a figure or object is transparent. Because there is no paper, there is no simulated print-through: do not render ink as translucent, do not blend inks into the empty area, and never use the cream paper colour as a background field. All shapes are fully OPAQUE flat colour. Depth comes only from overlapping opaque shapes plus one fixed lighter overlap tint - #E9DDF5 over blue, #FFE9B8 over yellow, #F6DCEC over pink, #D7ECD2 over green - never from gradients, shading, blur, glow or drop shadows. Palette: five inks maximum - peach #FFC2C3, butter yellow #FFDA7C, periwinkle blue #B9C5FA, hot pink #F45ED1, and grass green #A2DF92 which is rationed to a single "correct" signal. Cream #FFFCF6 may be used ONLY as the colour of specific objects (a card, a notebook, a speech shape, a slab) and never as a background field. Soft rounded organic shapes with slightly imperfect, hand-drawn edges; corner radius 8-14% of the short side. Line work is minimal: a single fine stroke of about 2px at final size, in a darker tint of the same hue or in the lightest cream, only for interior detail such as finger creases, fabric folds and facial features - never a dark outline, and never around the silhouette. Figures have natural proportions with exactly one deliberate exaggeration, varied body types, and deliberately non-naturalistic skin drawn from the pale inks (peach, cream, periwinkle, yellow). Faces are two dots and one line. Subtle paper grain and a faint halftone texture INSIDE the flat fills only, giving them a printed feel while the background stays perfectly transparent. Scattered confetti fillers: a squiggle, a dot, a short arc, a small cloud blob, 3-6px, at most eight of them, never touching the subject. Absolutely no lettering, numbers, glyphs, logos or UI text anywhere in the image. Subject: six adult learners seated in a loose circle around a low round table, seen from a slightly raised three-quarter view. Five of them are small, quiet and seated: shoulders slightly rounded, hands folded or resting flat, mouths closed, gazes turned down or away. The sixth is the exaggeration: seated in the same chair but drawn at nearly twice the relative size, leaning forward, chest open, one oversized hand raised mid-gesture, a large empty peach speech shape radiating from them in three concentric arcs that push the other five figures outward. The size difference is the only distortion. The table holds a single open notebook and two cups, no screens. Composition: the group fills the lower two thirds, the speaking arcs occupy the upper third, the top right is left as empty transparent space. The quiet five are one continuous cluster of overlapping blue, peach and yellow shapes; the speaker is the only figure in hot pink. Mood: a familiar room, quietly uncomfortable, not a disaster.
```
