\sld{1 \textbullet\ Title}
\say{One person talks, five people nod --- we built the opposite. I am [your name], this is [partner]. Five minutes: a problem, one rule, who pays.}

\sld{2 \textbullet\ The problem}
\say{Studying with friends beats any lecture, and it breaks in four ways.}

\sld{3 \textbullet\ Four ways it fails}
\say{One voice takes over; talking looks like learning. The simple question stays unasked. A wrong answer spreads. One explanation, six needs. The session ends busy, with no proof anyone learned --- and one question never got asked.}

\sld{4 \textbullet\ The tools we have}
\say{A chatbot answers in three seconds and thinking stops; helpful is not learning. Group apps cannot see three people lost, and the dashboard arrives too late. What is missing is not information. It is quiet time.}

\sld{5 \textbullet\ The solution}
\say{So we put that time in the software. One rule: the circle answers first, the AI answers second.}

\sld{6 \textbullet\ The mechanism}
\say{Peers get forty-five seconds, and Nova works unseen. Then the window closes, the peer answer is credited, and the AI answer lands cited. The AI waits because the software makes it wait --- not because we asked nicely. Delete it, and the session still runs.}

\sld{7 \textbullet\ One real turn}
\say{A learner types in Darija, no name. Two answers land; one says order does not matter. A quiet learner taps \textbf{me too} --- she never spoke. The checker catches the claim and opens a poll, so nobody is named. The cited answer arrives, the right peer gets credit. The AI checked the group; it did not replace it.}

\sld{8 \textbullet\ Four helpers}
\say{Four helpers, always labelled: a mediator runs the window, a tutor unlocks one hint at a time, a facilitator says who is stuck in sixty words, a teammate runs the quiz. That is the product.}

\sld{17 \textbullet\ Close}
\say{The best explanation in the room is usually not the AI's.}
\say{\textcolor{colB}{\textbf{B:}} So we make the AI wait, then measure whether the room learned.}
